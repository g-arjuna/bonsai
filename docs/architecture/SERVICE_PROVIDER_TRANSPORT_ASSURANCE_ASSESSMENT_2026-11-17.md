## 1. Goal and protocol landscape

Service-provider assurance must correlate physical/optical, link/LAG/QoS, IGP LSDB/TED, BGP RIB, MPLS/SRv6 FIB, LSP/policy/protection state, OAM measurements and customer service dependencies. A BGP session up, tunnel up, or graph path exists is not proof of delivery or SLA. Support coexistence and migration of LDP, RSVP-TE, SR-MPLS, SRv6, BGP labeled unicast and VPN/EVPN service labels; distinguish IP FRR (fast reroute) from FRRouting software. An IS-IS LSP is a link-state PDU, not an MPLS label-switched path. No automatic routing changes or signaling, PCE control or configuration writes.

## 5. Path, bottleneck and capacity algorithms

Extend BN-024 route-aware path engine, not another topology/path authority. Input headend/ingress, VRF/service/FEC, address family, class, source/destination, time/checkpoint, direction, path mode. Resolve ingress service label + transport recursion, LDP FEC, RSVP ERO/RRO and active path, SR policy candidate/list/BSID, prefix/adjacency SID semantics, per-hop local label operations push/swap/pop/PHP, ECMP/entropy, recursive BSID/SRv6 behaviors and limits. Respect local label namespace, VRF/table and AFI. Topology-only, computed SPF/CSPF, intended/configured, reported RRO, installed LFIB and observed probe are different modes. Use bounded DAG/branch set, loops/depth/cycle detection, explicit unknown/stale edges, exact time/scope, complete route and TED checkpoint. Do not infer all ECMP branches from one traceroute or one policy list.

Bottleneck workflow: (1) determine active path(s) and path changes, (2) join directional link/LAG member/class metrics in matching intervals, (3) rank candidates by sustained queue/drops, delay/loss, utilization, reservation headroom, optical and link-error evidence, (4) distinguish physical impairment, congestion, policing, path detour, scheduling, endpoint/OAM clock error, (5) report missing per-LSP allocation and path attribution. Link load is shared; no claim individual LSP caused it from port counters alone. For known serial path theoretical bottleneck capacity=min effective directional capacities, residual/headroom as appropriate; no guaranteed available service throughput; ECMP weighted branches need actual distribution or ranges, not sum of capacities by default.

Unused/underused link workflow: verify admin/oper and active bundle member, coverage and elapsed deltas; baseline across day/week/maintenance and seasonal patterns; classify intentional standby/protection, excluded by metric/affinity/Flex-Algo, unselected ECMP hash, LDP sync, failed/uninstalled, physical/MTU degradation, no demand, incomplete observations, candidate stranded capacity. Compute busy hour p95, headroom and demand correlation, compare both directions and parallel links, explain shortest path/TE constraint excluding link. No power-off/reconfigure recommendation from low traffic alone.

Capacity assurance: model demand matrix from verified per-service/LSP stats or sampled flow estimates with uncertainty, do not double count same traffic at multiple hops/label levels; use per-direction utilization, link class constraints, reservation vs actual load, oversubscription, N-1 link/node/SRLG/maintenance and shared protection competition. Estimate reroute distributions and resulting max utilization under stated assumptions, show unserved demand and coverage. No operational execution or automatic TE. Optional optimization suggestions only as clearly modeled what-if, separately authorized change workflow. Example: 100G link 90G load and alternate 100G link 10G; if first fails and all demand transfers, alternate reaches 100G before burst headroom, not evidence of actual failover or safe capacity. SRLG may invalidate both.

FRR investigation: protected link/node/SRLG and PLR, configured vs eligible vs installed repair, backup label stack and next hops, BFD/physical trigger, actual local repair event/packet loss, SPF/RIB/FIB convergence, reversion/reoptimization and microloop risk. Measure detection/repair/traffic recovery separately with clock uncertainty and probe sampling bound; no default 50ms promise. Test missing backup, node vs link protection, shared-risk failure, backup congestion and service impact.

## 6. Agent evidence and ML learning

Graph: existing Device/Interface/Site/VRF, BgpLsNode/Link, SrPolicy, MplsLsp, optical/rack relationships; extend directed IGP adjacency/TE edge, routing domain/area/level/algorithm, physical member/link/SRLG, label forwarding observation, LSP instance/candidate/segment list/repair, measurement session, service attachment and dependency intervals. Proposed read-only tools through shared MCP/HTTP registry: get_transport_health, trace_lsp (expected/reported/installed/probed), explain_lsp_bottleneck, explain_igp_convergence, explain_bgp_churn, assess_frr_protection, explain_unused_link, assess_capacity_risk, get_service_sla, assess_service_impact. Inputs typed, scoped and bounded; outputs evidence IDs, path/checkpoint and time skew, measurements/units/coverage, observed vs modeled, ranked hypotheses and counterevidence, next discriminating check. No arbitrary CLI or device writes, no unsupported causal proof. Reuse existing investigation and authorization/claim verifier.

Practical use cases and ML:
1. Chronic CRC/FEC growth at low load vs queue drops at busy hour; optical model cohorts, robust trends and labeled repair (BN-025).
2. Sudden latency step at stable reachability: compare OAM route/TE/LFIB time, queue, physical delay and clock state; distinguish reroute vs congestion.
3. BGP withdrawal storm or peer flap: per-peer/view churn rates, correlated BFD/IGP/physical root, RR versus downstream victims, independent service impact.
4. LDP session up but FEC missing/LFIB mismatch; RSVP preempt/reopt or SR policy invalid candidate/BSID; compare native forwarding and LSP echo.
5. Underused links, capacity asymmetry and N-1 hotspot, SRLG false diversity; deterministic constrained path first.
6. SLA anomaly and capacity forecast: seasonal robust baseline (hour-of-week), changepoint, quantile forecast and time-to-capacity threshold with intervals and censored data. Compare against naive seasonal/linear baselines.
7. Temporal edge/link/LSP features for supervised ranking or GNN, not just device degree: directional utilization/drops, error delta, PM delay/loss, IGP cost/metric change, repair state, route churn, service dependency and evidence freshness/missingness masks. Avoid leakage from post-incident data and simulator labels; group splits by site/time/device/failure, maintenance exclusions, clock/gap controls. Track precision/recall, false alerts/link-day, lead time, calibration, forecast pinball error, RCA top-k correctness, abstention, operator usefulness and resource cost. Keep labels independent (tickets, actual repair/failure, separate captures); synthetic replay tests logic, not field ML accuracy.
