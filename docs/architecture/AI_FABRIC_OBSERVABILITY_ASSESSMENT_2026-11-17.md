# AI factory / AI fabric observability research assessment

Date: 2026-11-17. Source audit baseline: main `fba050f5453ac3c7c4ad81840848dc23f6341f00`.
Status: design/intake, not implemented or live-qualified. Canonical execution queue: [BN-025 in BONSAI_BACKLOG.md](../../BONSAI_BACKLOG.md). Existing BN-008/009/013/016/019/021/022/023/024 ownership remains; do not create separate collector, identity, graph, auth or backlog systems.

## Decision and boundaries

Bonsai should provide a vendor-neutral, temporal evidence graph joining physical link quality, congestion, endpoint transport, workload and shared power/cooling dependencies. Scale-out Ethernet (RoCEv2 initially), scale-up (NVLink/NVSwitch, ESUN/UALink), InfiniBand and storage/management/front-end networks are separate fabric/transport profiles, not interchangeable counters. Keep Ladybug for structural topology, latest validated state, health summaries, incidents and durable evidence references; use bounded typed time-window storage or an authorized external TSDB for high-frequency metrics. No raw per-packet, perpetual QP or per-collective graph explosion. No operational device changes; do not implement transport protocols or automatically reroute. No new infrastructure by default. Secure Core, scoped reads and durability gates retain precedence.

## Research and technology directions

- [Google Protective ReRoute (SIGCOMM 2023)](https://research.google/pubs/improving-network-availability-with-protective-reroute/) changes IPv6 flow-label entropy in response to transport failure; reported 63–84% cumulative region-pair RPC outage reduction in its backbone study. [Cloud TCP PRR](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/networking/tcp-optimization-for-network-resiliency) is a TCP deployment option, not evidence of RoCE/UET behavior. Model path-selection changes even when RIB/BFD remain stable, distinguish application recovery from fabric repair, and look for old/new path evidence and timeout-to-recovery windows. Do not assume exporters expose PRR events or label changes.
- [UEC specification history](https://ultraethernet.org/specification-history/): 1.1 released 2026-10-12; [1.1 release overview](https://ultraethernet.org/ultra-ethernet-consortium-releases-specification-1-1-advancing-ai-and-hpc-networking/) adds scale-up, UET verbs compatibility, in-network collectives, CSIG and programmable congestion management. [UE transport overview](https://ultraethernet.org/ultra-ethernet-specification-update/) describes spraying, path avoidance and optional trimming. Distinguish wire/spec capabilities from counters actually exposed by a NIC/ASIC/firmware/API; no universal UET MIB or YANG assumption. Record UE profile, revision, congestion-control algorithm, optional feature flags, measured entropy/path evidence, CSIG bottleneck location and queue-delay/depth where present, trim/NACK/recovery, reorder, credit/window stalls, collective offload health. UET is not RoCEv2; Falcon is another transport; PFC may be absent, optional or profile-specific.
- [NVIDIA OCP Vera Rubin, 2026-10-12](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/ocp-global-summit-vera-rubin-scale-ai-factories/) describes MGX 1.2 liquid-cooled racks up to 250 kW, USFP and future Falcon transport support. [Spectrum-X, Aug 2026](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/giga-scale-ai-ethernet-evolution-spectrum-x-ethernet-rewrites-rules) motivates endpoint/switch co-observation, adaptive routing and hardware telemetry; vendor claims are not Bonsai test evidence.
- [OCP ESUN 1.0](https://www.opencompute.org/news/the-ocp-esun-10-specification-has-been-released/) has scale-up link retry, PFC and credit-based flow control. [UALink specifications](https://ualinkconsortium.org/specification/) include scale-up physical/transport and manageability; study NVLink/NVSwitch, UALink, ESUN separately. Link retry/credit starvation can mask physical or congestion faults from L3. [OIF implementation agreements](https://www.oiforum.com/technical-work/implementation-agreements-ias/) include CMIS 5.4 (May 2026), link training and coherent CMIS September 2026; [OCP Optics Reliability](https://www.opencompute.org/projects/networking/optics-reliability/) is developing common host/module diagnostics. Track CPO optical engines and external laser shared risk without assuming pluggable serial or DOM semantics.

## Operational interpretation and prediction

RoCEv2 is UDP/IP RDMA. ECN CE marking gives a receiver notification opportunity, CNP feedback drives sender rate control (e.g. DCQCN); PFC is per-priority hop-by-hop backpressure, not end-to-end congestion control. TX pause reports asking a neighbor to stop; RX pause is received backpressure; trace direction and priority mapping. Healthy ECN can mark without drops; high CNP alone is not failure. Sustained PFC time/headroom exhaustion/deadlock, drops and retry with job slowdown identify a problematic congestion episode; PFC watchdog drop may intentionally break a storm. A wrong DSCP→TC mapping can cause loss with healthy optics. Receiver incast and upstream victims must be separated, as must host PCIe/CQ/RNR issues. Normalize pause duty = delta duration / actual elapsed (same units; invalid if reset or impossible); ECN fraction only over compatible denominator; corrected-FEC rate not BER without valid bits denominator. Watermark peak is not average and no 1s scrape can recover unsampled microbursts.

Predict optical risk with module/model/firmware/FEC/temp-aware baselines, directional link pair and lane asymmetry, robust trend and change points, FEC histogram tail and uncorrectable events, bias vs temperature, loss margin relative to spec, flaps/retrains and corroborating congestion/retries. RX power alone cannot prove fibre fault; rising pre-FEC BER with stable power may be signal integrity; hot racks or shared laser can cause correlated faults. Track sensor quantization and limits, missingness, replacements and maintenance. Start deterministic risk ranking, then survival/hazard or calibrated ML only with real labeled failures/replacements and censored healthy periods. Compare simple thresholds vs robust baseline, precision/recall at lead-time windows, false alerts per link-day, calibration, held-out module/site/time and operational usefulness. Synthetic fault replay proves plumbing, not prediction. No universal dBm/BER threshold or promised time-to-failure.

## Graph and evidence design

Reuse BN-024 FactEnvelope and identity resolver. Proposed conceptual entities (not schema prescription): Fabric/Plane, existing Site/Rack/Device/Interface, PhysicalLink/Cable/Module/OpticalEngine/Lane (host/media side), Queue/PriorityGroup/BufferPool, Host/NICPort/GPU/PCI attachment, bounded Workload/CommunicationGroup allocation intervals, CoolingLoop/CDU/PowerDomain. Edges include component/port/lane mappings, directional link, queue classification/attachment, GPU-to-NIC path, workload allocation, shares cooling/power/laser/conduit, and time-qualified evidence links. One canonical authority per entity, not a parallel Device table. Current topology and current/summary health only; historical samples in a metric tier with immutable observations/checkpoints. Retire replaced entities and expired jobs/QPs with history ref, not delete structural evidence. Resolve alias changes and overlapping IPs/VRFs, multi-ASIC and breakout explicitly. Separate observed/inferred/configured/expected/simulated/verified, plus conflict and missing reason. No graph edge from untrusted exporter claims.

Path tool extends BN-024: return path set or bounded next-hop DAG by fabric/plane, direction, class, transport, interval, topology/RIB/FIB checkpoint; eligible vs actually observed paths, sampled fraction and method, entropy/weight if known, asymmetry, changes/repair and bottleneck evidence. ECMP, flowlet, adaptive routing, per-packet spraying and PRR are different modes. Never infer exact spray distribution or route from one traceroute, LLDP or flow sample. Compute potential blast radius of link/plane/power/cooling failure and remaining redundancy; label simulation and report unknown links.

Collection policy proposals (not device guarantees): inventory on change/slow (minutes), optics/thermal 5–30s baseline, queue/PFC/RDMA 1–10s where supported, high-resolution hardware histograms/watermark/INT events for microbursts; incident-triggered bounded faster read-only capture only at safe verified cadence. Source refresh cadence != scrape cadence. Keep raw incident windows, downsample long history preserving min/max/delta/count/coverage and histogram bounds; prioritize flaps, FEC uncorrectables, watchdog, job abort, power/leak and gaps. Explicit per-class budgets, rate, bytes, retention, source coverage and drop markers. Size using ports × lanes/queues × metrics × frequency plus host cardinality; benchmark actual compression/IO, graph amplification, clock skew and concurrent agent query p95. No scale certification from hypothetical arithmetic.

## Agent-facing investigation contract

Extend existing MCP/HTTP dispatcher and investigation runtime, typed bounded read-only tools (names illustrative): get_fabric_health, get_link_reliability, explain_congestion, get_rdma_endpoint, trace_fabric_paths, explain_collective_slowdown, get_shared_failure_domain, compare_fabric_state. Each returns subject/scope/interval, topology revision and metric watermarks, evidence IDs/units/deltas, freshness, coverage, observed vs modeled path, ranked hypotheses with support and counterevidence, missing discriminating check and uncertainty. Reuse existing access policy, redact raw payloads and protect against prompt injection. No LLM-derived authoritative edges or autonomous remediation.

Example replay: optical lane BER/tail worsens before corrected/uncorrectable FEC; link remains up, path distribution shifts and adjacent egress queue watermark rises; downstream PFC TX then upstream RX and endpoint CNP/retry increases; NCCL all-reduce p99 and step time increase. Agent can rank optical degradation→capacity imbalance→congestion hypothesis only if observations support time order and path/priority mapping, not assume causal chain from co-occurrence. Check alternate receiver incast, QoS mismatch, GPU throttling, NIC PCIe/CQ and stale telemetry. Return missing path attribution when spray evidence absent. Explicitly support a second case of congestion with healthy optics and third of optics degradation without workload impact.
